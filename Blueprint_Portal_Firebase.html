1.0 Site Administration ▼
Confirmed Pilot site: Tulsa 2 (TUL2) — Final assembly. Consumes transfers from TUL Main + ARV. Customer shipments. 6–10 outbound/week. Changed from Arvada in v2.2.
Confirmed 4 warehouses: ARV1 (Arvada), TUL Main (Tulsa 1 — metal fab), TUL2 (Tulsa 2 — pilot/final assembly), PONCH (Louisiana). Subsequent sites Phase 2+.
Confirmed 4 branches. Savant integration account requires full multi-branch access + Customizer permissions for inter-site transfers and cross-location transactions.
Confirmed 2 summary locations per site in Acumatica: WMS (sellable inventory) and QUARANTINE (non-sellable, awaiting inspection). Detailed bin-level locations in Savant.
Confirmed Zones: Receiving, Cage, Fabrication, Paint, Electrical, Assembly, Staging, Review/Quarantine, QC Hold. Role-based security via Savant Mobile + Admin Console.
Confirmed Arvada — South Shop (S Shop): Main fabrication and component preparation. Key areas: Metal fabrication & welding booths · Paint area for component finishing · Barn area storing sheet metal and copper · Industrial wiring and kitting zones · Under Mezzanine (staging area for parts, PDCs, switchboard inventory, electrical parts — requires forklift access) · Upper Mezzanine (secondary storage for spare parts and overflow materials) · Final assembly and outbound dock for internal transfers.
Confirmed Arvada — North Shop (N Shop): Manufacturing modular buildings and final electrical assembly. Key areas: Dedicated welding and electrical build areas · Racked storage organized by location for receiving and staging · Drop-off/staging zone for items arriving from South Shop. Typical inbound: HVAC units, wire spools, kitted electrical components. Buildings ~100 yards apart, single Acumatica warehouse ARV1.
Confirmed Tulsa operations: TUL Main and TUL2 ~15 min apart, modeled as separate Acumatica warehouses. TUL Main performs metal and copper fabrication. TUL2 handles final assembly including switchboards and top-level skid assemblies. Material flows TUL1 → TUL2. No customer shipments from TUL1. All final MOs and customer shipments completed at TUL2.
Confirmed Inter-site transfers: Two-step inventory transfers (ship → in-transit → receive). ~97% of transfers flow ARV → TUL. Reverse transfers (TUL → ARV) infrequent but follow same process. Sales Order Transfer model behaves as internal sale/purchase for full traceability.
Confirmed Security: Role-based access through Savant Mobile (handhelds) and Savant Admin Console (web). Users limited to modules authorized for their job function/security role.
2.0 Core Inventory & Configuration ▼
Confirmed Inventory profile: 366 Finished Goods, 9,302 Components, 763 Subassemblies. Sequential numeric IDs. MPN as Attribute (primary) + Cross Reference. Savant maps both.
Confirmed No serial/lot tracking in Phase 1. Future phases may add serial for high-value items. No non-stock items. No substitutions permitted.
Confirmed UPC & Cross Reference: Acumatica is master of record for both UPC and Cross Reference data. Savant syncs from Acumatica. UPC values must be unique per item for accurate tracking and identification.
Confirmed Product Attributes: Crusoe uses Acumatica Product Attributes — Manufacturer Name, Manufacturer Part Description, Manufacturer Part Number. These can be mapped to Savant WMS as needed.
Confirmed UOM: Wire managed in feet only, no LP per spool. Sheet metal purchased by weight, consumed as pieces. Savant handles all conversions.
Standard Putaway: Managed through Savant Mobile Putaway module. Zone-level rules based on zone attribute at item level in Acumatica. Direct-to-home-location when home location assigned. Location override available to operators. Replenishment: Traditional bulk-to-pick replenishment minimal at Crusoe — most materials put away directly into working zones. Home locations with Min/Max replenishment for small 2-bin section. Both directed and manual replenishment methods available in Savant.
Confirmed Labeling: Purchased products labeled by supplier. Subassemblies and finished goods labeled in-house. LP IDs (4×6 in.) assigned per pallet at receiving — consolidates multiple cases under single identifier for streamlined receiving, putaway, and picking. Stores item, quantity, and location data. Standard SKU labels printable during receiving or from Savant Mobile/Reprint Labels. Standard Savant LP, SKU, and product labels used. Modifications available via formal change request.
Standard Cycle counts: Driven by accounting department based on inventory value. Class A (top 80% of total value) counted monthly. Class B–D (remaining items) full count annually. Current process is manual using blind count sheets. Savant will support non-directed/manual count entry — results pushed to Acumatica for posting. Savant will not automate cycle scheduling.

Adjustment Flow

Operator → Quarantine→ Supervisor Adjustment (Savant)→ Issue Doc → Acumatica (Hold)→ Accounting Release
Confirmed Adjustments: All originate in Savant → push to Acumatica. Flow: Operator moves questionable stock to Review/Quarantine via handheld → Supervisor reviews and performs Adjustment in Savant → Savant creates Inventory Issue doc in Acumatica (set to Hold) → Savant immediately reflects qty change for warehouse visibility → Accounting reviews and releases for posting in Acumatica. Reason codes aligned between systems: BADDEBT, DAMAGED, INADJUST, INISSUE, INISSUEMFG, INRECEIPT, LANDEDCOST, MIGRATION, PARTADJ, PHYINVADJ, PPVADJ, TRANSFER, VENDORRET.
Confirmed Transfers: Sales Order Transfer model (Shipment → Receipt). Phase 1 mixed: TUL2 ships via Savant, ARV receives manually. Non-WMS→WMS per Addendum 006.
3.0 Receiving ▼

PO Receipt Flow

PO in Acumatica→ Scan PO # (Savant Mobile)→ Capture pallet by pallet→ Push receipt to Acumatica
Confirmed Receiving rules: Under-receiving permitted. Items not on original PO are not accepted. Nonconforming items received to maintain traceability. Heat numbers, inspection results, and supplier certifications uploaded under PO/Item Files tab in Acumatica — Savant will not manage attachments. Quality control managed through QMS (E-Workplace Apps).
Confirmed QMS Integration: When receiving items requiring QC, Savant automatically marks them with QMS Hold status — quantities not available for production or shipment. Once QMS (E-Workplace Apps) marks inventory as approved, a push notification from Acumatica/QMS automatically releases the hold in Savant. Change request: Configure push notifications from QMS to Savant for automatic QC release. Reason codes created in Savant. Full specification: Addendum 001.
Standard Putaway: Directed (zone rules, home location, override) + Non-directed (Product Move for items, LP Move for kits/pallets/skids).
Confirmed Vendor returns: Material moved to Review or Quarantine location in both Savant and Acumatica. Procurement and Quality teams notified. RMA (Return to Vendor) process initiated. Return processed as Purchase Return in Acumatica with corresponding one-sided inventory adjustment in Savant to reflect physical removal of material.
4.0 Production ▼
Confirmed MO flow: Savant WMS is the execution layer for production material flow and confirmations. Acumatica remains system of record. Process is fully barcode-driven and operation-based. MOs originate in Acumatica → flow automatically into Savant carrying full BOM hierarchy (raws + subassemblies). Savant creates separate work-center-level manufacturing orders from each Acumatica MO.
Confirmed Pick by Work Center: Operation-based pick tickets. Each Work Center (Fab, Paint, Electrical) gets only its relevant materials. See Addendum 002.
Confirmed LP for picks: Pre-kit into carts/racks. LPs stay in inventory until consumed. Standard Savant issue functionality. Backflushed materials: Addendum 003.
Confirmed Outsourced production: Outsourced copper parts follow Acumatica vendor shipment flow: (1) Scan and record materials sent to vendors (2) Receive finished parts back (3) Link to originating MO in Acumatica. Full specification: Addendum 004.
Confirmed Inter-site transfers: Subassemblies between ARV ↔ TUL scanned, logged, tracked. Production moves: Addendum 005. Transfers: Addendum 006.
Phase 2 Labor tracking: Operators scan ID + Work Order + Operation on Savant kiosks. Post-Phase 1 evaluation with production operators.
5.0 Order Fulfillment ▼

Fulfillment Flow

SO + Shipment (Acumatica)→ Manual release→ Pick (paper 8.5×11)→ Ship Confirm→ Auto-update Acumatica

Volume: ARV 5–6/wk · TUL1 4 internal/wk · TUL2 6–10 outbound/wk (increasing)

Confirmed 8.5" × 11" paper pick tickets. Standard Savant assignment. Operators scan barcoded shipment # on Savant Mobile/Picking.
Confirmed No QC/Packing module. Ship Confirm via Savant Mobile Ship Confirm module → confirmation automatically updates shipment status to Confirmed in Acumatica. Crusoe may print and distribute Savant Packing List to clients, detailing all pallets and their contents. Standard Savant labels/reports; modifications via formal change request.
Confirmed No customer returns process. Products are high-value, large-format equipment. Standard Savant Admin reporting.

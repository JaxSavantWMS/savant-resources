Site Administration

Warehouse Sites

Phase 1 focuses on a single pilot site. Subsequent phases extend coverage to all locations.

SMT-HQ
Headquarters
Fabrication, kitting, component prep. Two adjacent buildings (South & North) operating as one Acumatica warehouse.
SMT-FAB
Fabrication Plant
Metal and copper fabrication. Feeds components to the assembly site. No customer shipments originate here.
SMT-ASM Pilot
Final Assembly
Top-level assembly, finished goods production, customer shipments. Consumes inbound transfers from all other sites.
SMT-S
Southern Facility
Secondary production and storage site.

Integration Architecture

  • Four branches across all sites — Savant integration account requires full multi-branch access with Customizer permissions
  • Acumatica maintains two summary locations per site: WMS (sellable) and QUARANTINE (non-sellable, awaiting inspection)
  • Detailed bin-level locations maintained in Savant, mapped to Acumatica summary locations
  • Inter-site movement via two-step inventory transfers (ship → in-transit → receive)

Zones

Zones represent functional areas within each warehouse: Receiving, Cage, Fabrication, Paint, Electrical, Assembly, Staging, Review/Quarantine, and QC Hold.

Security

Role-based access through Savant Mobile (handhelds) and Savant Admin Console (web). Users are restricted to modules authorized for their job function.

Core Inventory & Configuration

Inventory Profile

Subassemblies
  • Sequential numeric Inventory IDs in Acumatica — synced to Savant
  • Manufacturer part numbers stored as Attribute (primary) and Cross Reference
  • No serial or lot tracking in Phase 1; future phases may add serial tracking for high-value items
  • Non-stock items not used; substitutions not permitted

UOM Conversions

Most items use a single UOM. Exceptions include wire/cable (managed in feet) and sheet metal/copper (purchased by weight, consumed as pieces). Savant handles all conversions.

Putaway & Replenishment

  • Zone-level putaway rules driven by item-level attributes in Acumatica Standard
  • Direct-to-home-location routing when home location is assigned Standard
  • Location override available to operators Standard
  • Min/Max replenishment for small 2-bin sections Standard

Product Labeling

License Plate IDs (4×6 in.) assigned to each physical pallet upon receiving. Standard Savant LP, SKU, and product labels used. Modifications available via formal change request.

Cycle Counts

  • Class A (top 80% by value): counted monthly
  • Class B–D: full annual count
  • Non-directed/manual count entry — results pushed to Acumatica Standard

Reason codes defined in Acumatica and aligned in Savant. Triggers include floor damage, lost/obsolete materials, and quality rejections.

Warehouse Transfers

Sales Order Transfer model (Shipment → Receipt) enables full traceability and inter-site accounting — behaves like an internal sale/purchase.

  • Both sites with WMS: Savant creates pick tasks, confirms shipment → Acumatica moves to In Transit → receiving site completes receipt
  • Phase 1 mixed: Pilot site ships via Savant; non-WMS sites complete receipts manually in Acumatica Addendum 006

Items requiring QC are automatically marked with QMS Hold status in Savant — unavailable for production or shipment. When QMS approves, a push notification from Acumatica releases the hold automatically.

Putaway Options

  • Directed: Zone-level rules, direct-to-home, with override capability
  • Non-directed: Product Move (item-level) or LP Move (kits, pallets, skids)

Purchase Returns

Material moved to Review/Quarantine → Procurement & Quality teams notified → RMA initiated → Processed as Purchase Return in Acumatica with corresponding Savant adjustment.

4

Production

Integrated Process

Savant operates as the execution layer for production material flow. Acumatica remains the system of record. Process is fully barcode-driven and operation-based.

  • Manufacturing Orders originate in Acumatica → flow automatically into Savant with full BOM hierarchy
  • Savant creates separate work-center-level manufacturing orders from each Acumatica MO Addendum 002

Pick Management

  • Operation-based pick tickets — each Work Center (Fabrication, Paint, Electrical, etc.) receives only its relevant material picks
  • Materials can be pre-kitted into License Plates (carts/racks) before issue; LPs stay in inventory until consumed
  • Backflushed material handling Addendum 003

Material Movement & Transfers

  • Production order moves between zones Addendum 005
  • Inter-site subassembly transfers scanned, logged, and tracked Addendum 006
  • Standard Savant functionality for material issuance

Outsourced Production

Vendor shipment flow: scan materials sent to vendors → receive finished parts → link to originating MO in Acumatica.

Addendum 004 Outside Process Vendor Shipment

Labor Tracking

Operator scan of ID + Work Order + Operation via Savant kiosks — planned for evaluation post-Phase 1. Phase 2
